Tuesday Tomato Rigatoni

30 minServes 4Vegetarian
Start cooking

Ingredients

  • Rigatoni400 g
  • Cherry tomatoes500 g
  • Garlic, sliced4 cloves
  • Olive oil3 tbsp
  • Basil, torna handful
  • Parmesan60 g

Method

1

Roast the tomatoes with the garlic and oil at 200°C until they burst, about 20 minutes.

2
